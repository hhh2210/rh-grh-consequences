# From RH to the prime-counting error

For $x\ge 2$, we seek $|\psi(x)-x|\le C\sqrt x\log^2 x$. The large-$x$ argument was formalized by Google DeepMind in Cryptids. The table identifies which estimates that argument supplies, where Mathlib enters, and where the two new deductions attach.

| Step | Input and output | Formal source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enumerate zeros | The ζ-zero set is closed and discrete, so its intersection with a compact rectangle is finite. The weighted count sums analytic orders over the nontrivial zeros in the rectangle. | [Mathlib’s zero set](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842/Mathlib/NumberTheory/LSeries/ZetaZeros.lean#L61-L71) · [Cryptids, bounded height](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/3647718b5df22e1a61462dec0376bd204526285a/Cryptids/Semantics/Riemann/VonMangoldtCount.lean#L1528) |
| Count globally and locally | Under RH a Jensen estimate bounds the weighted count up to height $T$. For the unit increment, cover the added zeros by the positive and negative height windows and sum their multiplicities. For $T\ge8$ this gives $(512/41)\log(T+4)$ and hence the stated big-O. | [Global estimate](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/3647718b5df22e1a61462dec0376bd204526285a/Cryptids/Semantics/Riemann/VonMangoldtCount.lean#L2042) · [Window Jensen bound](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/3647718b5df22e1a61462dec0376bd204526285a/Cryptids/Semantics/Riemann/VonMangoldtCount.lean#L1943) · [Unit increment](PsiZeroCountingWindow.lean) |
| Set the boundary estimates | The functional equation for $\zeta'/\zeta$ and a digamma estimate bound the vertical line $\Re s=-1/4$ by $\log(|t|+2)+85$. One can choose arbitrarily large ordinates $U$ with no zeros on the horizontal boundaries, and with a $C_h(\log U)^5$ bound there for $-1/4\le\Re s\le2$. | [Mathlib’s logarithmic derivative](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842/Mathlib/NumberTheory/LSeries/RiemannZetaLogDeriv.lean#L35) · [Vertical bound](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/3647718b5df22e1a61462dec0376bd204526285a/Cryptids/Semantics/Riemann/DigammaBinet.lean#L1585) · [Choice of heights](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/3647718b5df22e1a61462dec0376bd204526285a/Cryptids/Semantics/Riemann/HorizontalBounds.lean#L2102) |
| Shift a smoothed Perron integral | Evaluate the smoothed von Mangoldt sum as an integral, shift the contour, account for residues with their multiplicities, and bound the tail and horizontal errors. The terminal estimate uses the **global** count and the boundary estimates above. | [Perron for von Mangoldt](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/3647718b5df22e1a61462dec0376bd204526285a/Cryptids/Semantics/Riemann/WeightedStieltjes.lean#L1062) · [Contour shift](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/3647718b5df22e1a61462dec0376bd204526285a/Cryptids/Semantics/Riemann/WeightedStieltjes.lean#L3855) · [Final estimate](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/3647718b5df22e1a61462dec0376bd204526285a/Cryptids/Semantics/Riemann/WeightedStieltjes.lean#L5150) |
| Assemble the error bound | For $x\ge5000$ the published explicit inequality gives the coefficient $7/8$. On $[2,5000]$, the elementary Chebyshev upper bound and $\log x\ge1/2$ give a second constant. Choose the larger coefficient. Abel summation then gives the prime-counting bound. | [Large-$x$ arithmetic](https://github.com/google-deepmind/cryptids/blob/3647718b5df22e1a61462dec0376bd204526285a/Cryptids/Semantics/Riemann/ExplicitLarge.lean#L301) · [Small $x$](PsiCompact.lean) · [Assembly](PsiFromRH.lean) · [Abel summation](PrimeCountingFromPsi.lean) |

For a general Dirichlet-series route, Mathlib also provides `hasSum_mellin`, which exchanges a Mellin integral with a series under summability and integrability assumptions. The smoothed route above uses Cryptids’ specialized Perron identity. [Mathlib’s Mellin-to-series lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/fc4f59e577feb1a4956671bc04ac18aaaf8cd842/Mathlib/NumberTheory/LSeries/MellinEqDirichlet.lean#L28)
